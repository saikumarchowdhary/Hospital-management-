# CareLine HMS — Role and Permission Report

## 1. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Architecture
CareLine HMS enforces least-privilege access across 6 primary roles:
- **Administrator:** Full system access, audit log review, role permission configuration, and user management.
- **Doctor:** Patient consultations, clinical notes, diagnosis, prescriptions, and ward admissions.
- **Nurse:** Vital signs recording, nursing care plans, and patient monitoring.
- **Patient:** Personal medical records, appointment booking, and payment receipts.
- **Accountant:** Billing ledger reconciliation, invoice management, and financial reporting.
- **Pharmacist:** Medicine inventory, prescription dispensing, and stock batch tracking.
